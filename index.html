    About Drift

    Drift is a soundscape mixer for focusing, reading or falling asleep. There are no recordings: every sound is made live in your browser with the Web Audio API.

    • Rain is pink noise for the bed, with thousands of tiny filtered drops on top.
    • Thunder flashes first, then rumbles a few seconds later, like the real thing.
    • Wind, waves and fire are shaped noise that swells, gusts and crackles at random.
    • Crickets and birds are sine tones, chirped and swept into calls.
    • Keys wander through a pentatonic scale, so every note fits.

    The scene follows the mix: rain brings clouds, birds bring the dawn, crickets bring fireflies. Sounds keep playing in a background tab, and the sleep timer fades out over the last minute.
